🐾 🐶 Easy‑to‑Follow Puppy Care Tips ️

  • Provide clean water and healthy food every day
  • Give your puppy regular meals at the same time
  • Keep vaccinations and vet visits up to date
  • Offer gentle training with patience and love

🐕 Pet Safety Rules ️

Keeping pets safe is our top priority. Please follow these simple rules to ensure a happy and healthy life for your furry friends:

  • Always provide clean water and nutritious food
  • Keep pets in a safe and clean environment
  • Never leave pets unattended near roads or strangers
  • Visit a veterinarian regularly for checkups and vaccinations
  • Be gentle and kind—pets feel love and emotions too
  • Keep harmful items and medicines away from pets

🐾 Remember: A safe pet is a happy pet!

❓ Simple FAQs (Frequently Asked Questions) ️

Q1. Are pets suitable for children?

Yes, pets can be wonderful companions for children and help them learn responsibility and kindness.

Q2. How often should I feed my puppy?

Puppies usually need to be fed 3–4 times a day, depending on their age and breed.

Q3. Do pets need regular vet visits?

Yes, regular vet visits are important for vaccinations and overall health.

Q4. How can I make my pet feel comfortable at home?

Provide a safe space, toys, and lots of love to help them feel secure.

Q5. Why are pets called family?

Because they offer unconditional love, trust, and companionship—just like family ❤️
